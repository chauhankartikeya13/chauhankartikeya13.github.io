About

I am an AI/ML Engineer with a research-first foundation built at Delhi Technological University (DTU) — one of India's top engineering institutions. My work spans three intersecting domains: Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and Agentic AI systems.

What distinguishes my profile is that I contribute to AI, not merely consume it.
